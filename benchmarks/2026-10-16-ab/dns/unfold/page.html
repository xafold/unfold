What the figure leaves out

topicin a real resolver
13 servers per levelThere are 13 root names and 13 com. names. The resolver picks one by its measured round-trip time.
QNAME minimisationMany resolvers ask the root only com. NS?, not the full name (RFC 9156).
Other cachesThe browser and the operating system keep their own cache in front of the resolver.
Negative answersA name that does not exist (NXDOMAIN) is cached too, for a time set by the zone (RFC 2308).
No glueexample.org's servers are in cloudflare.com, so .org cannot send their address. The resolver looks them up first.
Left outAAAA (IPv6) records, DNSSEC, TCP fallback and retries after a timeout.
