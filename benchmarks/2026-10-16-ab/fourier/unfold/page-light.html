What the figure leaves out

topicwhat happens
cosine termsAll are 0 here, because f is odd: f(−x) = −f(x).
value at a jumpEvery S_N(0) is 0, the midpoint of −1 and +1.
kind of convergenceMean square: yes. Uniform: no, because of the overshoot.
complex formf = Σ c_k e^(ikx), with c_k = b_k / 2i and c_−k = −c_k for odd k > 0.
the limit 1.179(2/π)·Si(π), where Si is the sine integral. Found by Wilbraham (1848) and Gibbs (1899).
