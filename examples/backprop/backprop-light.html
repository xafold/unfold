Each node's rule

nodeforwardbackward (gradient sent to the left)
z = Wx + bweighted sum∂L/∂w = x · ∂L/∂z, and ∂L/∂x = Σ w · ∂L/∂z
h = σ(z)sigmoid∂L/∂z = ∂L/∂h · h(1 − h)
ŷ = v·h + cweighted sum∂L/∂v = h · ∂L/∂ŷ, and ∂L/∂h = v · ∂L/∂ŷ
L = ½(ŷ − y)²squared error∂L/∂ŷ = ŷ − y

What the figure leaves out

topicin a real network
many examplesThe gradient is the average over a batch of examples.
many layersThe same two rules repeat, layer by layer, from the output back.
other activationsReLU has slope 1 or 0. tanh has slope 1 − h².
vanishing gradientsσ′ is at most 0.25, so a product over many sigmoid layers becomes small.
matrix form∂L/∂W = δ xᵀ per layer, where δ is ∂L/∂z for the whole layer.
